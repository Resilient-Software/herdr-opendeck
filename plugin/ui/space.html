Herdr Space

A live tile for one Herdr workspace: its number in the corner, its name in the centre, the repository and ⎇ branch below. Press to focus that workspace in Herdr and raise the app hosting it. The footer shows FOCUSING… until Herdr confirms, or FAILED · TRY AGAIN briefly on error.

Key guide
Green Focused workspace.
Yellow An agent is working.
Red An agent is blocked and needs your input.
Plain Idle / done; the footer word carries the state.

Placement is the configuration: workspaces fill every key carrying this action, row-major, in Herdr's order. Leave the action off a key to reserve that key for something else.

With more workspaces than keys, the last two keys become ← and → pagers with a page indicator; the arrow pointing toward the focused workspace is accented. When everything fits again they return to being space tiles.

If Herdr is unreachable the deck shows HERDR OFFLINE, dims the last-known tiles, and goes inert until the next good snapshot.

This key has no settings.
